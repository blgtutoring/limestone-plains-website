How it works

From your first call to a report you can act on.

Every report follows the same four steps, and no number reaches you until a qualified reviewer has checked it.

  1. Tell us about it

    A short call about the property and what you want to do. You get a fixed scope and price before anything starts.

  2. We do the work

    Rules checked for your block, measurements taken, options costed and the report drafted from tested templates.

  3. A reviewer checks it

    A Certified Quantity Surveyor signs off the costs, and a Registered Planner signs off the planning reads.

  4. You get the answer

    A clear report with the answer on page one, one follow-up question included, and a list of who to engage next.

Questions

Before you book.

Is this a valuation or a quote?

Neither. A quote is a builder's price to do the work, and a valuation is a registered valuer's view of what the property is worth. We sit before both: an independent estimate of what each option should cost and whether it stacks up, so you know what a fair quote looks like before you ask for one.

Do you lodge development applications?

We don't lodge them, but we tell you whether you're likely to need one and what it involves. If you go ahead, a town planner or building designer prepares and lodges it.

Can you recommend a builder?

We give you a shortlist of the kinds of people to contact, and local firms if you ask. We never endorse a single company and take no fees from anyone on the list.

I haven't bought yet. Is it too early?

That's the best time. The Pre-Purchase Check is built for buyers, with a 2–3 business day turnaround that fits before an auction or within the cooling-off period.

Do you come to the property?

Most checks are done from the plans, the title, government mapping and photos. Where a site visit changes the answer, we include one and say so in the scope.

Who checks the numbers?

A Certified Quantity Surveyor reviews every cost estimate, and a Registered Planner reviews the planning reads, before the report reaches you.
