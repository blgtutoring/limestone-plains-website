Who we are

Limestone Plains Advisory (ABN [00 000 000 000]) is a Canberra business providing building cost and feasibility advice. Contact: [email@limestoneplains.com.au].

What we collect

When you enquire or book, we collect your name, email, phone number, the property address, and the documents you choose to send us, such as plans or builder's quotes.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry, scope and prepare your report, invoice you, and answer your follow-up question. We don't sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

The people who prepare and review your report: our team and our reviewing Certified Quantity Surveyor and Registered Planner. They use it only for your report. We may use anonymised cost figures, with nothing that identifies you or the property, to improve our estimates.

How we store it

We keep your information in secure cloud services and keep reports for [7] years for our records and insurance. You can ask us to delete information we no longer need to keep.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you by emailing us. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, tell us first; you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
